Before You Apply

Who can apply, and what you'll need

Please read this carefully — having everything ready speeds up your approval.

Who can apply

You may apply if you are a salaried employee with a regular salary paid into your bank account. We will review your income, documents and payment ability before approval.

Guarantor rules

Two guarantors are required. Each guarantor must be a salaried employee, must provide correct documents, and must sign the guarantor section of the form. We may call the guarantor or employer to verify the information.

Important: Please provide true and complete information. Approval is not guaranteed. Do not hand over original CNICs. Keep a copy of the completed form and your payment schedule.

Documents you must attach

  • One recent passport-size photograph of the applicant
  • Clear copy of the applicant's CNIC, front and back
  • Copy of the applicant's employee card — or an employer letter if no employee card
  • Salary bank statement for the last 12 months from the account where salary is received
  • Salary slips for the latest 3 months, if available
  • Post-dated installment cheques from the same salary bank account, as required by the selected plan
  • Clear copy of CNIC and employee card for each guarantor
  • Salary proof for each guarantor — latest salary slips or bank statement
